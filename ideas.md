# Predictive Risk Intelligence Platform — Design Direction

## Three Approaches

### Theme Name: Control Room Noir
**Very Brief Intro:** A dark, operational command center with restrained amber alerts and cyan telemetry. Built for analysts who need signal, hierarchy, and high-confidence decisions under pressure.
**Probability:** 0.06

### Theme Name: Ledger & Lattice
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm ivory editorial system inspired by institutional research reports, financial ledgers, and precision grid work. Calm, credible, and highly legible.
**Probability:** 0.08

### Theme Name: Signal Meridian
**Very Brief Intro:** A cool, high-contrast observability interface that combines data-viz geometry, glassy surfaces, and a single electric chartreuse signature. Technical without becoming cyberpunk.
**Probability:** 0.04

## Chosen Approach: Ledger & Lattice

### Design Movement
Swiss International Typographic Style blended with modern financial research terminals: strict typographic hierarchy, asymmetric information bands, restrained ornament, and a tactile paper-like surface.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence before decoration:** Every visual flourish should clarify a system relationship, metric, or decision path.
2. **Asymmetric confidence:** Use offset panels, ruled lines, and staggered columns to make a dense platform feel composed rather than boxed in.
3. **Institutional warmth:** Ivory, ink, oxidized teal, and a precise saffron signal create trust without looking generic or clinical.
4. **Operational clarity:** Status, risk, and pipeline state are always scannable within one glance.

### Color Philosophy
The base is mineral ivory rather than white, creating the quiet authority of a printed annual report. Ink-black type establishes governance and precision. Oxidized teal marks trusted data movement and healthy services. Saffron is reserved for attention, drift, and decision points so that risk reads as deliberate rather than alarming.

### Layout Paradigm
A persistent left rail anchors navigation while the main canvas unfolds as a sequence of offset bands: executive signal strip, architecture map, risk model cards, and pipeline runbook. Content is intentionally not centered; visual weight shifts left-to-right like a research note being annotated in real time.

### Signature Elements
- Fine rule lines and small uppercase metadata labels, used as an institutional annotation system.
- A clipped-corner “signal tab” motif for live platform states and pipeline events.
- A faint square lattice texture behind architecture and telemetry sections.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like operating a trusted internal instrument: direct, calm, and reversible. Hover states lift cards by a few pixels and reveal metadata; tabs change context without visual whiplash; buttons use concise verbs such as “Inspect run” and “Open model card.”

### Animation
Use 180–240ms ease-out transitions for hover, focus, and panel reveals. On first load, stagger major information bands by 50ms with opacity and translateY only. Telemetry lines may pulse once on load, but never continuously distract. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and preserve instant keyboard navigation.

### Typography System
Display: **DM Serif Display** for page titles and key numeric callouts, lending the platform an editorial research voice. Body and UI: **IBM Plex Sans** for compact reading, tables, labels, and controls. Use uppercase 10–11px tracking-heavy labels for metadata, 14–16px body copy, and 44–72px display numbers only when the metric is the dominant decision signal.

### Brand Essence
A governed decision layer for financial risk teams that turns Azure data products into explainable action, without hiding the operational details. Personality: **measured, exacting, quietly confident**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are declarative and specific; CTAs are short and operational; microcopy states what changed, what is trusted, and what needs attention. Avoid hype and generic onboarding language.

Example lines:
- “A risk signal is only useful when its lineage is visible.”
- “Inspect the feature path before promoting the score.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a compact split-square glyph: two offset ledger cells joined by a diagonal signal notch, suggesting a data table becoming a decision. It appears beside a custom wordmark set in a serif display face with a small teal signal bar.

### Signature Brand Color
**Oxidized Teal — #0F6B68.** It owns the brand because it sits between financial blue and operational green: trustworthy, technical, and unmistakably tied to governed movement through the platform.
